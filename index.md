## ❓ Troubleshooting

- **The game won’t start** – Make sure your copy of *ASTRO BOT Rescue Mission* is valid and that you’ve pointed AstroQuest to the correct folder. Also check that your headset is connected and recognized.
- **VR is not detected** – For PC streaming, confirm Virtual Desktop or SteamVR is running and that your Quest 3 is paired. For standalone mode, ensure your headset has the latest system update.
- **Performance is choppy** – Lower the graphics settings inside AstroQuest. On PC, close background applications and update your GPU drivers. On Quest 3, make sure no other apps are running in the background.
- **Controllers not working** – Recalibrate your controllers in the Quest 3 settings, then restart AstroQuest. Some users may need to adjust the controller mapping inside the emulator.
- **Audio issues** – Check that your headset is set as the default audio device. For PC streaming, enable audio passthrough in Virtual Desktop or SteamVR.

If problems persist, visit the GitHub repository’s Issues section to see if others have found solutions.
